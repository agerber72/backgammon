## Android and Windows app

The app works offline. It contains no functions that send data over the internet, and the Android version does not request permission to access the internet.

On your device, the app stores:

- the state of the running game,
- your settings, that is color, level, pace, forced moves and language,
- the dice statistics,
- the leaderboard of your games against the computer with date, level, color, play time and points.

This data does not leave your device, and we have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.
<!-- ende -->

<!-- nur: android -->
On Android, this data is excluded from device backups and from transfer to a new device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
<!-- ende -->

<!-- nur: windows -->
On Windows, this data is stored in the file `%APPDATA%\Achim Gerber\Backgammon\shared_preferences.json` in your user directory. The file remains after the app is removed; you can delete it yourself. If you use a roaming Windows profile, for example at work, your employer may synchronize this folder with the profile.
<!-- ende -->

## Web version

The web version is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the USA. When you open it, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the page and to protect it against attacks.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering the web version securely and without a server of our own, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We consider this interest to prevail, because only the technically necessary data is processed and we ourselves receive or evaluate no logs.

GitHub decides how long it stores this data; we have no access to it. Data may be transferred to the USA. According to GitHub, such transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses. GitHub's privacy notice: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The web version stores nothing in your browser, neither cookies nor local storage, and keeps no leaderboard. The game, settings and dice statistics exist only while the page is open. They are lost when you close or reload it.

## Your rights

Insofar as we process personal data about you, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability under the respective legal conditions. Please contact us at the e-mail address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg).
